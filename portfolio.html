Tactile Browser

An IEEE Xplore-published project for IEEE Student Product Initiative: a tactile, two-dimensional browser that helps blind people explore the internet. It targets a 37× price reduction compared with the closest competitor and will be presented at IEEE MWSCAS 2026.

Volumetric Display

Won 2nd place at MakeMIT x Harvard's entertainment track, the largest hardware hackathon in the US with 400+ participants. Collaborated with a team to build a volumetric display by rotating an led matrix at high speeds, creating persistence of vision.
